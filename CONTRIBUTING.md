# Contributing

Everyone is welcome to question, discuss and propose improvements. Open an issue describing the principle, problem, evidence and proposed change. Submit a pull request with the rationale and potential unintended consequences. Use respectful language; do not publish private information.

Use the review record template in the [Four-Check Amendment Review Protocol](AMENDMENT-REVIEW.md). Assess life and dignity, whole-Accord consistency, misuse and reversal, and autonomy and accountability. Each check must pass before adoption. Re-run the checks after substantive revisions. Independent human review is required for substantive amendments; AI analysis is advisory only.

No principle or creator is beyond scrutiny.
