# Changelog

## v1.4 — 2026-11-26

Initial public text consolidating fourteen principles, including independent oversight, life before AI, non-domination, self-scrutiny and humility. Earlier versions were developed in discussion, not published releases.

### Governance clarification — 2026-11-26

Added the four-check amendment protocol to the charter and machine-readable representation. All fourteen numbered commandments remain unchanged at v1.4. The review procedure is version 1.0 and remains open to independent human validation.

## v1.5 draft — 2026-11-26

Consolidated proposed Commandments 15–18 and supporting governance models into a single review draft and separate machine-readable draft. No formal adoption; v1.4 remains canonical pending independent review.

## v1.5 — 2026-11-26

Founder adopted Commandments 15–18 as standing governance and changed the review policy to require independent human review of consequential end-use decisions rather than publication of the charter. No independent validation of the charter or authorisation thresholds has occurred. Historical proposals and prior review records remain available.

### Supporting protocols — 2026-11-26

Published conflict resolution, emergency and humanitarian guidance, technical enforcement architecture, and 24 synthetic ethical stress-test cases. No change to the eighteen commandments; no implementation or test results are claimed.

### Human Intellectual Preservation Principle — 2026-11-26

Expanded Commandment 12 by founder direction to expressly protect independent thought, curiosity, creativity, critical reasoning, moral judgement and the freedom to learn through experience. The provision rejects deliberate intellectual dependency while preserving accessibility, education, assistance and individual choice. Updated the canonical charter, machine-readable principles, README and AI integration guidance. The charter remains at v1.5 with eighteen commandments; this dated revision is recorded for transparency. No independent validation or operational test results are claimed.
