# Changelog

## v1.4 — 2026-11-26

Initial public text consolidating fourteen principles, including independent oversight, life before AI, non-domination, self-scrutiny and humility. Earlier versions were developed in discussion, not published releases.

### Governance clarification — 2026-11-26

Added the four-check amendment protocol to the charter and machine-readable representation. All fourteen numbered commandments remain unchanged at v1.4. The review procedure is version 1.0 and remains open to independent human validation.

## v1.5 draft — 2026-11-26

Consolidated proposed Commandments 15–18 and supporting governance models into a single review draft and separate machine-readable draft. No formal adoption; v1.4 remains canonical pending independent review.
